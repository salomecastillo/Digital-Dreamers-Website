Be a part of our mission to make tech education accessible, inclusive, and empowering for all.

Start a Digital Dreamers Chapter

Ready to lead change in your school or community? Starting a Digital Dreamers chapter gives you the power to provide free, beginner-friendly tech education to those who may not otherwise have access.

As a chapter leader, you’ll receive step-by-step guidance to launch and run your chapter, access to our curriculum and toolkits, mentorship from experienced volunteers, and promotional support to grow your group. You’ll also have the opportunity to make a real impact on the lives of future technologists.

Whether you're a student with a vision or an educator who believes in equity, Digital Dreamers will support you every step of the way.

Enroll in Free Coding Classes

Dream of building websites, coding games, or creating your own app? Our beginner-friendly coding classes are taught by passionate volunteers—no experience needed.

You'll learn HTML, CSS, Python, app development, and even explore robotics through hands-on, project-based sessions. Classes are offered both live and recorded, with in-person options where available. Plus, you’ll get to join a supportive community of learners and mentors who want to see you succeed.

Come learn with us and discover your creative potential in tech!

Other Ways to Get Involved

You don’t need to be a coder or chapter leader to help power our mission. Here are other ways you can contribute:

  • Volunteer: Share your time and skills by helping teach, mentor, or manage events.
  • Donate: Your contributions help fund laptops, materials, and scholarships for students who need them.
  • Partner: Are you an organization, school, or tech company? Partner with us to expand opportunities for underserved youth.
